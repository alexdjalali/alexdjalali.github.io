  <h2>Professional Software Engineering for AI and Distributed Systems</h2>
  <div class="project-tech-list">
    <span class="project-tech">Python</span>
    <span class="project-tech">FastAPI</span>
    <span class="project-tech">Docker</span>
    <span class="project-tech">PostgreSQL</span>
    <span class="project-tech">RabbitMQ</span>
    <span class="project-tech">OpenTelemetry</span>
  </div>
  <p>
    A graduate course on the difference between writing code and engineering production systems: fifteen weeks, two sessions each, with a lab, a handout and graded homework every week. Students inherit Orion, a deliberately broken distributed AI platform (a FastAPI service, an async worker, PostgreSQL, Redis, a message queue and a language-model layer), and over the semester make it reproducible, typed, layered, tested, resilient, observable, fast, secure and evaluated, then defend the result in front of a panel.
  </p>
  <p>
    The slides, labs, rubrics and reading list are public, and Orion is a template repository you can copy and evolve. The solutions, the answer keys and the reference implementation are kept private.
  </p>
