## AI product and engineering

I built the natural language technology behind [Tableau's Ask Data](https://www.tableau.com/products/add-ons/natural-language) and hold 9 patents in natural language interfaces for data visualization. In 2023 I co-founded [PerceptivePanda](https://web.archive.org/web/20250119063219/https://www.perceptivepanda.com/) as CTO; [Zapier](https://zapier.com/) acquired it in 2025. The [projects](/projects/) page walks through the systems, and [Six Months of Enterprise Development with AI](/posts/six-months-of-enterprise-development-with-ai/) shows how I build with agents today.

## Enterprise AI transformation

At [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/) I led cross-cloud analytics integration and AI strategy. I also teach teams to work with AI: an onsite [applied AI course](/courses/) on Microsoft 365 Copilot, and a graduate course in [professional software engineering](/posts/professional-software-engineering-for-ai-and-distributed-systems/). [Rethinking Agile in the Age of AI](/posts/rethinking-agile-in-the-age-of-ai/) sets out what changes in roles, review and planning.

## Board and executive advisory

Advice on AI strategy for boards and leadership teams, drawing on the product, platform and research work above: a [Stanford PhD in Linguistics](/about/), [published research](/publications/), and two products taken to acquisition.
