## 1. Purpose

Make sure hours are recorded accurately and overtime is approved before it is worked.

## 2. Timekeeping

1. Employees record time daily in the timekeeping system, by work order number where one exists.
2. Timesheets are submitted by **1200 Monday** for the previous week.
3. Supervisors approve timesheets by **1700 Monday**.
4. Corrections after approval require a correction form signed by the employee and supervisor.

## 3. Overtime

1. **All overtime must be approved in advance** by the employee's supervisor. There is no amount of overtime that can be worked without approval.
2. Exception: overtime during an Emergency response (FM-SOP-07) may be worked without advance approval. The supervisor approves it by the next business day.
3. Requests for more than 10 overtime hours in a week for one employee also need the Business Manager's approval.

## 4. Records

Approved overtime requests are kept with the timesheet.
