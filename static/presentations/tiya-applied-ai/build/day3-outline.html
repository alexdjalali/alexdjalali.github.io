Day 3 Outline: Advanced Agents with Copilot Studio

The third day of Applied AI at TIYA Services. Day 2 built agents that wait for a person to ask. Day 3 builds agents that start themselves: on an incoming email, on a schedule, and by handing work to other agents. It also covers how to run them safely once no one is watching.

1

Audience and prerequisites

  • Who: TIYA staff who completed Day 2 and will design or own agents for their team: facilities leads, the work order desk, business management, and supervisors. About 30 per cohort, with limited remote seats.
  • Prerequisite: Day 2, Agentic AI with Microsoft Copilot. Day 3 assumes participants can write agent instructions from the template, write tests before building, and rate an agent's risk tier. It opens with a 15-minute Day 2 review and doesn't depend on any agent built on Day 2.
  • Accounts: a Copilot Studio license and maker access to a training environment (section 5).

4

The four builds

Each build adds one capability on top of the last, using the same fictional SOPs and work order data as Day 2. Step-by-step instructions are in the Day 3 workbook.

Build 1 · 8:30

SOP Navigator (Studio)

  • Rung: 3, on-demand
  • Knowledge: the four sandbox SOPs
  • Teaches: the Copilot Studio workspace, settings as locks, publishing for reuse
  • Human check: the person asking
Build 2 · 10:00

Work Order Intake

  • Rung: 4, event-triggered
  • Trigger: a new email with [WO-TRAIN] in the subject
  • Tools: post to Teams, to the owner only; no send-email tool
  • Teaches: payloads, untrusted input, tools as locks
  • Human check: a Facilities Lead reads every card
Build 3 · 12:30

Monday Backlog Briefing

  • Rung: 4, scheduled
  • Trigger: recurrence; 5 minutes for testing, then Monday 7:00, then off
  • Tools: read the Excel table, post to Teams
  • Teaches: schedules, cost, checking AI arithmetic
  • Human check: the lead verifies numbers before sharing
Build 4 · 1:30

Facilities Desk

  • Rung: 5, orchestrated
  • Agents: SOP Navigator (connected), Priority Advisor and Notice Drafter (child)
  • Teaches: routing by description, child vs. connected, when not to split
  • Human check: the person asking; notices are drafts

5

Environment requirements

Day 3 needs more of TIYA's tenant than Days 1 and 2 did. Confirm each item with TIYA IT, in writing, at least 14 days before.

  • A Copilot Studio license for every participant and both instructors, and maker access to one training environment.
  • Which cloud TIYA is on (commercial, GCC, or GCC High), and that event triggers, agent flows, and child and connected agents are available there.
  • Data policies allow the Office 365 Outlook, Microsoft Teams, Excel Online (Business), and SharePoint connectors in the training environment.
  • Enough Copilot Studio capacity for the day. Estimate: 30 participants × (8 inbox + 3 red-team emails + about 6 scheduled runs + about 20 test messages per build).
  • The AI Training Sandbox SharePoint site from Day 2, with the four SOPs, still in place with read access for every participant.
  • A plan to switch off or delete every training agent and trigger after the day.
If Copilot Studio isn't available

Run Builds 2–4 as instructor demos on screen. Participants complete the trigger canvas, triage log, routing tests, and runbook on paper from the demo output. Build 1 can run in the Copilot agent builder instead. The outcomes shift from "build" to "design and evaluate".

6

Assessment and certificate

  • Formative: an eight-question knowledge check, self-scored. Instructors collect anonymous per-question scores.
  • Evidence of work: the triage log from Build 2, the red-team log, and the runbook.
  • Certificate (proposed): full attendance (up to 30 minutes excused), Build 2 run against the sample inbox, a completed runbook, and every trigger shown turned off before leaving.

8

Decisions for the program owner

  1. Date. Day 3 has no date yet. Every Day 3 document shows "[Date TBD]".
  2. Copilot Studio access. Day 3 depends on it (section 5). If TIYA can't license participants, decide between instructor-demo delivery and postponing.
  3. Who attends. Day 3 suits people who will own agents, not everyone who took Day 2. Consider a smaller cohort of 15–20.
  4. Who approves triggered agents at TIYA. The closing asks participants to name the person who would approve a triggered agent. TIYA should name that role before the day.
  5. Certificate criteria. Confirm the proposal in section 6.
