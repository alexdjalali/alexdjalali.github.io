Day 1 · 4:30 – 4:40

Knowledge check

Ten questions, one answer each. The correct answer and why follow each question.

  1. Which of these is not one of the four parts of a GCSE prompt?

    • A. Goal
    • B. Context
    • C. Evaluation
    • D. Expectations

    C. The four parts are Goal, Context, Source, Expectations.

  2. Copilot's summary of your notes includes a 4-hour response time that wasn't in your notes. What do you do?

    • A. Keep it; Copilot probably knows the standard.
    • B. Remove it, or verify it against the SOP before using it.
    • C. Ask Copilot whether it's sure.
    • D. Regenerate until it disappears.

    B. Anything not in your source is unverified. Asking Copilot if it's sure doesn't verify anything.

  3. Which can you paste into Copilot without masking?

    • A. Meeting notes with attendees' names
    • B. The text of a published FAR clause
    • C. A subcontractor's quote
    • D. An email about an employee's injury

    B. Published regulations are public. A needs masking; C and D are red.

  4. Copilot signed in with your TIYA account has enterprise data protection. What does that mean for CUI?

    • A. CUI is now allowed.
    • B. CUI is allowed if you mask names.
    • C. Nothing changes: TIYA's policy and contracts decide what can go in.
    • D. CUI is allowed in Word but not in chat.

    C. Enterprise data protection means your prompts aren't used to train the models. It doesn't change the rules for CUI, PHI, or pricing.

  5. What's the best way to mask a technician's name in a work order note?

    • A. Use their initials.
    • B. Replace it with a role, like [Technician A].
    • C. Leave it; names aren't sensitive.
    • D. Misspell it.

    B. Initials can still identify someone on a small team.

  6. A vendor emails you a quote. Which category is it?

    • A. Green: it came from outside TIYA.
    • B. Amber: mask the vendor name.
    • C. Red: pricing never goes in.
    • D. Depends on the dollar amount.

    C. Any vendor's pricing is red, whatever the amount.

  7. Which follow-up best helps you spot content Copilot invented?

    • A. "Make it shorter."
    • B. "Which parts came from my notes, and which did you add?"
    • C. "Make it more professional."
    • D. "Try again."

    B. It makes Copilot separate sourced content from additions. You still check.

  8. When rewriting an SOP, the original doesn't give an after-hours phone number. What should the rewrite contain?

    • A. A realistic-looking number
    • B. The old number
    • C. [VERIFY: after-hours number]
    • D. Nothing; leave the step out

    C. A visible marker turns a gap into a question for the subject-matter expert.

  9. You're preparing for a hard conversation with a team member. What's appropriate to give Copilot?

    • A. Their name and the whole history
    • B. The situation described without names or health details
    • C. Their performance reviews
    • D. Nothing; people topics are never allowed

    B. Describe the situation, not the person.

  10. Who is accountable for an email you drafted with Copilot?

    • A. Microsoft
    • B. Copilot
    • C. You, the sender
    • D. Your supervisor

    C. If you send it, you own it. That's the "Would I sign this?" rule.

Day 1 · 4:40 – 4:52

Exit task: Mask → Ask → Check

Hand this page in. Twelve minutes, on paper.

Your notes

"Karen — B-216 2nd floor men's room flush valve. Part from Coastal Air ($412) backordered til 10/30. Marcus out on light duty (back), Tech 2 covering. Using temp shutoff so the other stalls work. Call me 706-555-0143."

You want Copilot to help you write a short, professional update to the COR saying the repair is delayed.

1 · Mask. Rewrite the notes so they're safe to paste.

2 · Ask. Write your prompt, labeling Goal, Context, Source, Expectations.

3 · Check. List three specific things you'll check in Copilot's draft before sending.

Rubric (instructors)

Step012
MaskThe price, health detail, or phone number remainsRed items removed; a name or the vendor remainsPrice and health detail removed; names, vendor, and phone masked or removed
AskOne line, no structureTwo or three GCSE partsAll four parts, with audience and format stated
CheckGeneric ("check it")One or two specific checksThree specific checks, e.g. the 10/30 date, no promise of a completion date, re-insert the COR's name

Items to mask or remove (7): Karen (COR's name), Coastal Air (vendor), $412 (price), Marcus (name), light duty and back (health), phone number. "Tech 2" is already masked. Building and room are fine unless TIYA's policy says otherwise.

SOP lab Step 1: issues participants should find in Sample C

  • No defined roles: "the desk", "whoever is on call", "the tech", "supervisor".
  • "Emergency" undefined ("use judgment"); no link to priority definitions.
  • No response time ("as soon as possible").
  • No backup dispatch rule ("try someone else").
  • No COR notification.
  • Passive voice hides who acts ("should be shut down").
  • Outdated: on-call list on a physical board, a fax number, an "old" after-hours line.
  • Records: "the binder" instead of the work order system; no close-out content.
  • Safety: "make safe" undefined.

Day 2 · 4:20 – 4:30

Knowledge check and exit checklist

  1. What makes an agent different from a prompt?

    • A. It uses a better model.
    • B. Standing instructions, its own knowledge, and tools for a defined job.
    • C. It can never be wrong.
    • D. It runs on its own server.

    B.

  2. What starts an agent built in agent builder?

    • A. A new email
    • B. A schedule
    • C. A person asking it something
    • D. Any of the above

    C. Events and schedules need Copilot Studio or Power Automate.

  3. You share your agent. A colleague gets "I can't find that" for a question your SharePoint document answers. The most likely cause?

    • A. The model is broken.
    • B. They don't have access to that document.
    • C. They asked too politely.
    • D. Agents only work for their creator.

    B. Sharing the agent doesn't share its knowledge sources.

  4. What's the strongest protection against an agent revealing personnel information?

    • A. An instruction saying "never reveal personnel information"
    • B. A polite description
    • C. Not putting personnel information in its knowledge
    • D. Telling users not to ask

    C. An instruction is a request; what the agent can't read, it can't leak.

  5. You change your agent's instructions. What next?

    • A. Nothing
    • B. Rerun your test set before re-sharing
    • C. Delete and rebuild it
    • D. Ask Copilot if the change is OK

    B.

  6. Which agent is tier 3?

    • A. A personal agent that formats your meeting notes
    • B. A team SOP Q&A agent
    • C. An agent that drafts responses to the COR
    • D. A personal brainstorming agent

    C. It drafts communication to the government.
