Day 2 Outline: Agentic AI with Microsoft Copilot

The second day of Applied AI at TIYA Services. Day 1 taught participants to prompt Copilot safely. Day 2 teaches them to build no-code agents with the Copilot agent builder: standing instructions, their own knowledge, and tools for one defined job. Participants test each agent before sharing it, and govern it after.

Audience and prerequisites

  • Who: TIYA staff in operational and administrative roles who completed Day 1. About 30 per cohort, with limited remote seats.
  • Prerequisite: Day 1, Generative AI for Daily Work. The current cohort took Day 1 in June 2026. Day 2 opens with a 10-minute Day 1 review, and nothing later depends on a Day 1 exercise or sample.
  • Accounts: a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, or at least Copilot Chat, with the agent builder enabled (section 5).

4

The builds

Two guided builds on fictional TIYA SOPs and work order data, then the participant's own agent. Step-by-step instructions are in the Day 2 workbook.

Build 1 · 10:45

SOP Navigator

  • Architecture: knowledge Q&A
  • Knowledge: the four sandbox SOPs
  • Teaches: the instructions template, tests first, "I can't find that" over guessing
Build 2 · 11:25

Work Order Analyst

  • Architecture: multi-tool
  • Knowledge and tools: FM-SOP-03 plus code interpreter, with an 80-row CSV uploaded in chat
  • Teaches: analysis with tools, and checking the numbers by hand
2:00

Build your own

  • From: the participant's design canvas
  • Teaches: five tests before building, partner swap test, safe publishing
  • Then: red-teamed and governed in the last block

5

Environment requirements

Confirm each item with TIYA IT, in writing, at least 14 days before.

  • Every participant has a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, or at least Copilot Chat. Licenses from the June Day 1 are still active.
  • Which cloud TIYA is on (commercial, GCC, or GCC High), and that the features below are live there.
  • The agent builder ("Create an agent" in Copilot Chat) is enabled for participants.
  • Agent sharing to specific users is allowed.
  • Code interpreter and file upload are enabled in Copilot Chat.
  • An "AI Training Sandbox" SharePoint site with the four sample SOPs, readable by every participant, at least a week before.
If Copilot is down or blocked

The lead walks through saved build screenshots on screen, and pairs complete the design canvas and test plan on paper.

6

Assessment and certificate

  • Formative: a six-question knowledge check, self-scored. Instructors collect anonymous per-question scores.
  • Evidence of work: the test plan, the partner's swap-test log, the risk tier, and the governance card.
  • Certificate (proposed): full attendance (up to 30 minutes excused), at least one agent built and tested with five tests, and a completed governance card.

8

Decisions for the program owner

  1. Outcome 2 vs. the agent builder. LO2 promises autonomous agents and the agenda lists event triggers. The agent builder makes on-demand agents; triggers and autonomous agents need Copilot Studio, which is Day 3. Day 2 teaches triggers as a design concept with an instructor demo. Either soften LO2 ("design, configure, and deploy agents, and design event-triggered automations") or provide a Copilot Studio environment for the demo.
  2. Closing. The agenda ends at 4:30 with no closing block. The exit checklist, evaluation, and certificates run from 4:20 inside the governance block. If the day can run to 5:00, move them to a 4:30–5:00 closing.
  3. Session headers. The agenda's sessions are titled only "Morning" and "Afternoon". Suggested: "Agent Foundations & First Builds" and "Deploy, Trigger & Govern".
  4. Certificate criteria. Confirm the proposal in section 6.
